**Authorized Contributor** means an employee, contractor, officer, or other individual identified in Schedule A whom you have authorized to Submit Contributions that you own or control.

**Contribution** means any original work of authorship, invention, modification, addition, documentation, design, test, configuration, data, media, or other material that an Authorized Contributor intentionally submits on your behalf for inclusion in, or use with, a Project.
